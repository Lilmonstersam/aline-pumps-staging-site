About Aline Pumps

Who We Are

Aline Pumps has spent over 30 years engineering and supplying pump and water storage systems for Australia's commercial and infrastructure sectors. We're 100% Australian owned and operated, and our systems are trusted on projects including Sydney Opera House, Westfield Sydney CBD, Bunnings, and the Australian Government.

We work closely with hydraulic engineers, contractors, developers, and facilities managers from project inception through to ongoing maintenance, because we know that time, safety, cost, and quality all matter in this industry.

We're pumping with innovation.

Why Aline Pumps

The Integrated Approach

Aline Pumps offers an end-to-end, cradle-to-grave approach which we call The Integrated Approach. At Aline Pumps, buying a pump is just the beginning of the relationship. We support the full lifecycle with quality products, expert technical guidance, genuine spare parts, preventative maintenance, and responsive service.

  1. 1

    Design Support / Value Engineering

    We provide technical information, product guidance and value engineering support to help consultants and specifiers identify suitable pumping solutions.

  2. 2

    Product Assembly / Testing & Supply

    We supply reliable pump systems across domestic water, wastewater, fire water and specialist applications, supported by dependable delivery and product knowledge.

  3. 3

    Commissioning / Operational Testing

    We support commissioning and operational testing to help confirm systems are installed, operating and performing as intended before handover.

  4. 4

    Preventative Maintenance / Spare Parts

    We provide scheduled maintenance programs designed to reduce downtime, extend equipment life and support reliable long term performance.

  5. 5

    Replacement / Upgrade

    As requirements change or assets reach end of life, we help customers plan practical replacements and upgrade options for long term value.
