    StormWarden: An Overview

    Home  /  News & Insights  /  StormWarden: An Overview

    Stormwarden System · 2021-03-19

    At Aline Pumps, we not only provide a wide range of fire pumps, but we also offer a range of power backup systems, specifically suited to providing alternate power to submersible systems. Flooded basements due to a power outage (thus disabling the pump systems) are one of the primary causes of environmental vehicle damage. The Aline StormWarden system provides a solution to this issue providing automatic cut-in power, ensuring your assets stay safe and functional:
    • Lift wells
    • Wine cellars
    • Basement car parks
    • Garages
    • Workshops
    • Below ground storage
    • Entertainment areas
    The range of StormWarden Systems is matched with our range of submersible stormwater pumps to ensure your property and contents are protected, all the time, every time.

    The StormWarden Battery Backup System

    When mains power fails, standard stormwater pump systems will cease to function. To prevent this, Aline stocks a specifically designed battery backup system called StormWarden®. The battery backup system is a packaged unit incorporating an inverter with a bank of batteries to provide electric power for stormwater systems in the event of mains power failure. A purpose-built control unit detects a power failure and the system automatically switches supply to the pump set to battery power until the mains power is restored. The systems are available in a range of sizes to cater to specific requirements, depending on pump size and required standalone runtime. The intelligent controller provides continuous monitoring of mains power and automatically switches to battery power in the event of a mains power failure. It then automatically reverts to mains power and begins recharging the batteries when the power supply is restored.

    The StormWarden® Diesel Generator System

    A StormWarden® Diesel Generator System is a great choice for off-grid power production or as an emergency power backup system for your critical pump sets. Our engines are known for their reliability and low maintenance, giving you peace of mind. StormWarden generators are well equipped under the hood, powered by a refined three or four-cylinder industrial engine that you can always depend on. With earth leakage protection to Australian standards and fully equipped with fail-safe engine protection, the generator is as safe as it is dependable. Perfect for any application requiring a 415V backup power supply, a StormWarden® Diesel Generator can be specified to suit your exact pump power needs and will guarantee peace of mind for years to come.
