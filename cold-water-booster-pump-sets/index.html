    What Is a Water Pressure Booster Pump?

    A water pressure booster pump increases water pressure and flow in buildings where mains supply is insufficient. These pumps draw water from a storage tank and use high-speed impellers to push it through the system, delivering consistent pressure to taps and appliances across all levels.

    How Does a Booster Pump Work?

    A cold water booster pump system operates in the following way:

    1. Water is fed from a mains-supplied break tank.
    2. The pump activates when pressure drops below a set point.
    3. Impellers spin, increasing water velocity and pressure.
    4. Water is delivered to fixtures throughout the building at stable pressure levels.

    Booster pumps are essential for:

    • Multi-storey residential buildings
    • Hospitals, commercial buildings, and hotels
    • Smaller homes with poor municipal supply

    What Is a Variable Speed Pressure Pump?

    A variable speed booster pump adjusts its motor speed to meet changing water demand. It maintains stable pressure while using less energy and reducing wear compared to fixed-speed alternatives.

    Advantages of variable speed systems:

    • Energy efficient operation
    • Quiet, smooth pressure delivery
    • Long equipment life
    • Ideal for fluctuating usage patterns
