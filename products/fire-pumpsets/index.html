Where they are used

Common Applications

High-quality fire hydrant pump systems are essential tools with versatile applications including fire protection during emergencies, efficient water transfer between locations, and precise irrigation for agricultural needs. They are also used for washing down equipment and facilities to maintain cleanliness, and for dust suppression in construction or industrial settings. These applications make fire pumps invaluable for emergency services, farmers, and civil contractors, providing reliable performance and rapid deployment when needed most.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the three major types of fire pumps?
The two main types of fire pumps are diesel and electric centrifugal pumps, known for their durability and ease of maintenance, ideal for high-flow applications. Vertical Turbine Pumps are the next major group, used to draw water from below-ground sources like wells or tanks and suitable for negative suction pressure, compact and space-saving, designed for installations with limited room.
Where are fire pumps installed?
Fire pumps are installed into residential and commercial buildings in Australia and must meet strict standards, including fire-resistant construction, especially for buildings over 50 meters. They also need clear access for maintenance, proper ventilation to prevent overheating, and drainage systems to handle water discharge. Compliance with AS 2941-2013 ensures the installation is reliable and effective, meeting all performance and safety standards.
Who designs fire pump systems?
Fire pump systems are designed by fire protection consulting engineers who calculate size of systems by the overall area the pump systems are required to protect, along with taking into account water source provisions including pressure requirements and volume criteria.
