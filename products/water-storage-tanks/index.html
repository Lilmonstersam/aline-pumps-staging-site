    Where they are used

    Common Applications

    Water storage tanks, particularly Aquamate modular steel tanks, serve diverse roles across industrial and commercial environments. They are essential for fire protection, providing a reliable water source for suppressing fires in various settings. These tanks also support agricultural activities through water storage for irrigation, ensuring consistent crop watering. In industrial processes, they supply water necessary for manufacturing and cooling functions. Additionally, they store drinking water in areas with limited access to municipal supplies and act as emergency reserves during water supply disruptions. Furthermore, water tanks are utilised for collecting and using rainwater for non-potable purposes like irrigation and facility maintenance.

    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the life expectancy of a water storage tank?
    The life expectancy of a water storage tank depends on its material and maintenance. For example:
    • Plastic/Polyethylene Tanks: Typically last 10–20 years with proper care.
    • Steel Tanks (Coated or Galvanised): Can last 30–50 years or more, especially with regular maintenance to prevent corrosion.
    • Fibreglass Tanks: Often exceed 50 years due to their resistance to rust and environmental factors.
    Regular cleaning, inspections, and adherence to maintenance schedules significantly enhance the lifespan of any tank.

    What size are industrial water storage tanks?

    Industrial water storage tanks are available in a wide range of sizes to accommodate various needs:

    • Small Industrial Tanks: 5,000 to 50,000 litres, suitable for smaller facilities or specific applications.
    • Medium Industrial Tanks: 50,000 to 250,000 litres, commonly used in manufacturing and agriculture.
    • Large Industrial Tanks: 250,000 litres or more, often utilised in mining, municipal water storage, and large-scale industrial operations.

    Custom sizes and configurations are also available to meet specific industry requirements.
