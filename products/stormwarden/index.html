StormWarden Backup Power Systems for pumps

Aline's StormWarden backup power system is designed for high reliability and automatic operation during power outages. Each system is pre-tested and manufactured with high-quality components, including Australian-made inverters and chargers.

Read more

The automatic cut-in power ensures smooth transitions, providing instant backup when the mains fail, and reverts to the primary power source once it's restored. The system incorporates automatic battery charging when connected to the mains, ensuring it's always ready for emergencies. Additionally, the StormWarden uses robust lithium batteries with built-in safety features like overcurrent protection and low-voltage cut-off, preventing damage from overuse or deep discharge. This comprehensive package includes all the necessary components, from the submersible pump to the inverter and charger, for a seamless installation and reliable operation.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is an industrial UPS system?
An industrial Uninterruptible Power Supply (UPS) system is designed to provide consistent and reliable power to critical industrial operations during power outages or disturbances. Unlike commercial UPS systems, industrial units are built to endure harsh environmental conditions such as extreme temperatures, humidity, and dust. These systems are commonly used in industries like manufacturing, oil and gas, and chemical processing, where power disruptions can cause significant safety and operational issues.
How do I calculate backup power requirements?
Calculating backup power requirements involves determining the total power consumption of the equipment connected to the UPS. Start by listing all devices and noting their power ratings in watts (W) or volt-amperes (VA). Sum the total power usage and add a 25% safety margin to account for surges or future needs. Then, determine the required runtime during outages to choose a UPS with adequate battery capacity. For example, if your total load is 384W, a UPS with at least 480W capacity, including the safety margin, would be appropriate.
How does a commercial UPS work?
A commercial UPS system ensures uninterrupted power to connected devices during outages. It includes a rectifier, battery, and inverter. Under normal conditions, the rectifier converts AC power to DC to charge the battery, while the inverter supplies power to the equipment. During a power failure, the battery provides DC power to the inverter, which then delivers AC power to maintain operations without interruption.
How long can UPS last without power?
The runtime of a UPS during an outage depends on its battery capacity and the connected load. Small UPS systems may provide power for a few minutes, enough for a safe shutdown, while larger systems can sustain operations for several hours. Manufacturers often offer runtime charts or calculators to help users estimate backup durations based on specific load requirements. Selecting the right UPS ensures adequate power support for the desired duration.
