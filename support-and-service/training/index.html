Product training sessions

Practical pump training for your team

Equip your team with in-depth knowledge of pumping equipment through Aline Pumps’ Talk & Tour or Lunch & Learn sessions. Designed for hydraulic engineers, cadets and trainees, the sessions provide hands-on exposure to products, assembly and testing against Australian standards.

Talk & Tour or Lunch & Learn

Build your team’s understanding of how pumping equipment works in commercial building services. Sessions can include a facility tour to show products, assembly and testing in practice.

Topics We Can Cover

Fire Pumps

Learn about Aline Pumps’ fire pump and enclosure offerings, including suitable locations and applications.

Sewage & Stormwater Pump Stations

Understand the operation and function of packaged pump stations for specific projects.

Submersible Pumps

An overview of vortex, dewatering, drainage, cutter, grinder and macerator pumps.

Water Storage Tanks

Explore the design and function of modular steel water tanks.

Pressure Systems

Learn about pressure boosting pump solutions for residential and commercial applications.

Trade Waste Management

An introduction to general purpose pits, oil-water separators and grease arrestors.

Filtration Process Training

Discover filtration products that can be customised for site-specific requirements.
