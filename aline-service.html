With over 30 years of experience and a dedicated team of qualified technicians, Aline Service supports the pump and water systems in your building for the long haul through tailored maintenance packages, fast emergency response and the manufacturer knowledge that comes from having built the systems ourselves.

Why Aline Service

Built-in knowledge. Ongoing care.

01 / EXPERTISE

Manufacturer Expertise

We built your system, so we know it best. That means faster diagnosis and fewer callbacks.

02 / RECORDS

Asset Tracking

Every site and visit is logged in our integrated Asset Tracking system, so we can flag potential issues before they become failures.

03 / RESPONSE

24/7 Emergency Response

Our service technicians can respond to critical failures after hours, on weekends and on public holidays.

04 / PARTS

Backed by Our Facilities

Our manufacturing and warehousing operation keeps spare parts and replacement systems ready when you need them.

Planned care

Ongoing Maintenance

Our maintenance packages are tailored to your site, with scheduled visits designed to catch potential issues before they become critical failures. Whether you have a single system or a full building portfolio, we build a plan around what is on site.

When it matters most

Emergency Call-Outs

Equipment failures happen. Aline Service technicians are available for emergency call-outs at any time, including after hours, weekends and public holidays, so a critical failure never has to wait until Monday.

What we work on

Systems We Service

We service a wide range of hydraulic systems, including:

  • Dual stormwater pumpsets
  • Dual sewer pumpsets
  • Liftwell pumpsets
  • Carwash and oil-water separators
  • Dual pressure pumpsets
  • Dual rainwater pumpsets
  • Dual hot water circulators
  • Fire and sprinkler pumps
  • Cold water pressure systems
  • Filtration systems
  • Above and below ground tanks
  • Submersible pumps
Scheduled servicing

What’s Included in a Maintenance Visit

Every scheduled visit is recorded against your asset file.

  1. Physical inspection of all pump units and controls
  2. Operational testing under load conditions
  3. Control panel checks and alarm verification
  4. Lubrication, minor adjustments and repairs
  5. Photographic records uploaded to your asset file
