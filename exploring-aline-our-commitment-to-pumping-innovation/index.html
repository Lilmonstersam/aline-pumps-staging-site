    Exploring Aline: Our commitment to pumping innovation

    Home  /  News & Insights  /  Exploring Aline: Our commitment to pumping innovation

    Aline News · 2020-02-14

    We’ve long been committed to offering our clients quality-focused pumping solutions that are designed for both residential and commercial applications. Since we kicked off our operations in 1995, our team has worked hard to establish a reputation built around top-tier standards, further solidifying our position in the industry. So, what exactly is Aline all about, and what can we offer you as a loyal customer?

    Our specialties are spread far and wide

    The team at Aline are well-versed in a variety of pumping and filtration solutions across:
    • Industrial estates
    • Commercial properties
    • Property-related developments
    • Hospital infrastructure
    • Mining developments, and more.
    Focusing on building these services on a customer-centric foundation, we consider ourselves a trusted, first-choice supplier across the market.

    Meet our team

    We wouldn’t be where we are today without our highly efficient team of experts who streamline our success each and every day. Providing fire protection pumps and advice that puts you in the best position towards fulfilling your needs, we’ve helped more than 1,500 properties with their requirements. From working with the Australian Government to servicing the likes of Sydney Opera House – we’ve dabbled in projects of all walks of life. And we believe that’s what makes us a distinct player in the field.

    Okay, so what’s our purpose?

    Aline is wholly focused on offering a dynamic range that covers an industry that’s constantly evolving. Through strategic innovation and a commitment to transparency, our offering is a key factor in the success of projects small and large. Bridging gaps across the industry, our pumping solutions and product range solves problems for each of our customers. Our range (to name a few) includes:
    • Fire protection pumps
    • Fire fittings and protection
    • Plumbing systems and accessories
    • Water storage tanks
    • Engine controllers.
    Get in touch to find out more If you’re not sure what system of product best suits your needs, we encourage you to get in touch with the Aline team on 1800 018 999.
