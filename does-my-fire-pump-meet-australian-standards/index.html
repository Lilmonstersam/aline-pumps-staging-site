    What Is AS 2941? Understanding Fire Pump Standards in Australia

    Home  /  News & Insights  /  What Is AS 2941? Understanding Fire Pump Standards in Australia

    Aline News · 2023-02-03

    wp:paragraph

    AS 2941:2013 is the Australian Standard that specifies the mandatory requirements for designing, constructing, installing, and maintaining fire pump sets in fixed fire protection systems. Compliance with AS 2941 ensures that fire pumps deliver the necessary flow rate, pressure, and reliability to safeguard properties during emergencies. Every fire pump installed in Australia must meet this standard to be legally compliant.

    /wp:paragraph wp:paragraph

    Key Takeaways:

    /wp:paragraph wp:list
      wp:list-item
    • Choosing an AS 2941-compliant pump ensures legal compliance and operational safety, guaranteeing sufficient water supply in fire events.
      • /wp:list-item wp:list-item
    • AS 2941:2013 sets the design and performance standards for all fire pump installations, covering critical factors like flow rate, suction, discharge, and pressure capacity.
      • /wp:list-item wp:list-item
    • Fire pumps must be certified and labelled to confirm compliance with AS 2941, including clear manufacturer documentation and the Australian Standards mark.
      • /wp:list-item
    /wp:list wp:heading

    Checking a Fire Pump

    /wp:heading wp:paragraph

    To ensure a fire pump meets Australian Standards:

    /wp:paragraph wp:list {"ordered":true}
      wp:list-item
    1. Check the manufacturer’s specifications: Fire hydrant pump manufacturers should provide detailed information about the products.
      2. /wp:list-item wp:list-item
    2. Check the pump’s certification: A certified fire hydrant pump will have a label indicating compliance. This should confirm the pump meets AS 2941:2013.
      3. /wp:list-item wp:list-item
    3. Look for the Australian Standards mark: Products that meet the Australian Standard will display a mark or logo indicating this.
      4. /wp:list-item
    /wp:list wp:heading
