    What are ESFR Sprinklers and how they can be used in warehouses?

    Home  /  News & Insights  /  What are ESFR Sprinklers and how they can be used in warehouses?

    News & Insights · 2020-04-13

    Fire Protection
    wp:paragraph

    Early Suppression Fast Response (ESFR) sprinkler systems are specialised fire protection systems designed to suppress high-challenge fires in warehouses and storage facilities. Unlike traditional systems that aim to control fires, ESFR sprinklers are designed to suppress them quickly at the source. Positioned at the ceiling, these high-volume sprinklers eliminate the need for in-rack systems and provide greater flexibility and fire protection.

    /wp:paragraph wp:paragraph

    Key Takeaways:

    /wp:paragraph wp:list {"ordered":true,"start":1}
      wp:list-item
    1. ESFR sprinkler systems suppress fires early, reducing fire growth and minimising damage.
      2. /wp:list-item wp:list-item
    2. They eliminate the need for in-rack sprinklers, offering easier storage reconfiguration and fewer risks of accidental discharge.
      3. /wp:list-item wp:list-item
    3. Designed for high-piled storage and demanding warehouse environments, ESFR systems require high water supply and proper pump infrastructure.
      4. /wp:list-item
    /wp:list wp:heading

    How ESFR Sprinklers Work

    /wp:heading wp:paragraph

    ESFR systems activate quickly during the early stages of a fire, releasing a high volume of water—up to 380 litres per minute per sprinkler head. Their suppression ability is based on three critical metrics:

    /wp:paragraph wp:list
      wp:list-item
    • Response Time Index (RTI): Speed of sprinkler activation
      • /wp:list-item wp:list-item
    • Actual Delivered Density (ADD): Water delivered to the top of the burning material
      • /wp:list-item wp:list-item
    • Required Delivered Density (RDD): Water needed to suppress the fire
      • /wp:list-item
    /wp:list wp:paragraph

    Suppression occurs when ADD exceeds RDD in the early moments of a fire.

    /wp:paragraph wp:heading

    Applications of ESFR Systems in Warehouses

    /wp:heading wp:paragraph

    ESFR systems are ideal for:

    /wp:paragraph wp:list
      wp:list-item
    • High-piled storage: Palletised goods, rack storage, shelf storage
      • /wp:list-item wp:list-item
    • Warehouses with frequent layout changes
      • /wp:list-item wp:list-item
    • Facilities storing a variety of commodities—from non-combustibles to plastics
      • /wp:list-item
    /wp:list wp:paragraph

    Because they’re ceiling-mounted, ESFR sprinklers simplify warehouse design and remove the need for costly in-rack sprinkler reconfiguration.

    /wp:paragraph wp:heading

    Advantages of ESFR Sprinklers

    /wp:heading wp:list
      wp:list-item
    • Wide Coverage: Suitable for a variety of commodity classes
      • /wp:list-item wp:list-item
    • Early Fire Suppression: Stops fire spread faster than conventional systems
      • /wp:list-item wp:list-item
    • Flexible Storage Design: No need for in-rack heads, allowing easier layout changes
      • /wp:list-item wp:list-item
    • High Output: Uses high-pressure, high-volume discharge for rapid suppression
      • /wp:list-item
    /wp:list wp:heading

    Limitations and Considerations

    /wp:heading wp:list
      wp:list-item
    • Selection must be based on the type of commodity and storage method
      • /wp:list-item wp:list-item
    • Not suitable for open-top containers, as water cannot reach lower rack levels
      • /wp:list-item wp:list-item
    • Requires large water supply and fire pumps to maintain adequate pressure and volume
      • /wp:list-item
    /wp:list wp:heading {"level":3}

    Choose the Right ESFR Solution with Aline Pumps

    /wp:heading wp:paragraph

    While ESFR systems may solve many of the more challenging problems in warehouse protection against fires, ensuring that the correct type of ESFR is used involves a full understanding of the commodity and methods of storage in the warehouse. Careful consideration must also be given to the long-term use and range of flexibility expected by the owner of the building.
